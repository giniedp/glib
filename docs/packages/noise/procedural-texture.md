# Procedural Texture

Bakes a noise field into a texture on the GPU and draws it to the screen.
Pick a preset to switch between noise graphs, some of them colored with a gradient. The _Uniforms_ sliders tweak the noise at runtime, the _Texture_ options change the baked region and animate the noise over time.
